    Total return

    Everything your investments made since your first trade: price gains on what you still hold, gains you locked in by selling, plus dividends and interest, minus fees charged outside trades.

    Money you put in or took out does not count as return.

    Performance

    Profit is your total return on each day. Value is what your investments were worth; it also rises when you add money.

    Compare shows what the same money would be worth if it had gone into the S&P 500 (an index of 500 large US companies).

    Beta: how much your portfolio moves with the US market. 1 = the same, 0.5 = half as much.

    Interest on your cash

    Compares the interest rates of your bank and savings accounts and shows where moving money earns more. For an account that updates itself, the rate comes from the account: the rate the provider states, or the one worked out from the interest it paid (that figure is approximate). A rate you enter in Settings always wins. Only moves within the same currency, so you take no currency risk.

    Keep an everyday buffer where you bank. Trading 212 pays interest through money-market funds: very low risk, but not covered by a bank deposit guarantee. Not financial advice.
